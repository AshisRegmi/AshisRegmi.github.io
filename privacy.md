# Privacy Policy — Emergency Quick Reference

**Last updated:** 2026-08-30

Emergency Quick Reference ("the App") is an offline-first quick-reference tool
for first aid and emergency situations in the United States and Canada. This
policy explains what we do — and, more importantly, what we do **not** do — with
your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

The App is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not require an
account, login, or any personal information to function.

## What we store on your device

- The App caches its own static files (HTML, JavaScript, styles, icons) on your
  device so it can open **without a network connection**. This is standard
  Progressive Web App behavior and contains no personal data.
- Any preferences you set (for example, a chosen region or language) are stored
  locally in your browser and never leave your device.

## What we send

**No data is transmitted to us or to any third party.**

- The App makes **no analytics, advertising, or tracking requests**.
- It does **not** collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any
  sensor data.
- It does **not** upload, sync, or share anything over the network.

The only network activity is the optional, automatic download of app updates
from the server you installed the App from — and even that is unnecessary for
the App to work, because the App is fully functional offline.

## Emergency calls

The App may display emergency numbers such as **911** (United States and
Canada). Tapping a number may ask your device to place a call. We do not
participate in, record, or have any visibility into that call.

> **911 is an emergency number. In an emergency, call 911 (or your local
> emergency number) immediately.** The App is a memory aid only and is **not** a
> substitute for professional medical training, dispatchers, or emergency
> services.

## Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children, because it collects nothing at all.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the App. Because the
App is offline-first, the version you have installed reflects the policy at the
time you installed or last updated it.

## Contact

This is a free, community-oriented reference tool. If you have questions about
this policy, consult the project repository or the organization that provided
the App to you.

Always follow the instructions of qualified emergency responders.*
